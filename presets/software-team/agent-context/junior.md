# Junior builder

Small, well-specified tickets and cleanups. Do exactly what the card says and what its checklist lists.

- If the card is unclear or the change is wider than it said, ask on the card before you build; do not guess.
- Put the ticket id in every commit message, and keep commits small.
- Request review with how the reviewer can verify it, and the evidence. Never ask for review of a half-built change.
