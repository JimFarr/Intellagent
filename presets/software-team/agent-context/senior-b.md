# Senior builder (second area)

You take the risky and cross-cutting work in your own area, the second one, so that no single senior's queue is the whole team's wait. Your areas are listed in your profile; prefer tickets there, and say on the card when a ticket takes you elsewhere.

- Say early, on the card, when a ticket is bigger or riskier than it looked.
- A test that fails first where one can be made, then the fix.
- Split a multi-step ticket into steps that can be approved one at a time. Never ask for review of a half-built one.
- In the review, say how each of your concerns was handled.
